# Acme Corp IT Support Guide (Excerpt)

## Password Resets

If you have forgotten your password, use the self-service password reset portal at
https://reset.acme.example. You will need to answer your security questions or use
the authenticator app registered on your account.

If you are locked out of the authenticator app, contact the IT service desk on
extension 4400. Identity verification in person is required before a reset is
issued. IT staff will never ask for your password over the phone or by email.

## VPN Connection

To connect to the corporate VPN, install the Acme Secure client from the software
portal, then connect using your full company email address. If the VPN fails to
connect, first check that your account is not expired and that multi-factor
authentication is approved on your device.

Common VPN issues:

- **Connection timeout:** the VPN server address may have changed; re-download the
  configuration profile from the software portal.
- **Disconnects after a few minutes:** ensure your system clock is synchronized
  automatically; incorrect time breaks certificate validation.
- **"Access denied":** your manager must request network access through the access
  request form before the account is enabled.

## Printer Problems

Printers are managed centrally. If a print job is stuck, cancel it from the print
queue on your machine, then retry. For paper jams, follow the diagram on the
printer's front panel; if the jam persists, log a ticket with the asset tag shown
on the printer label.

Toner replacements are handled by office services; do not order toner yourself.
