# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Annual Leave

All full-time employees receive **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis calculated from their contracted
hours. Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in
advance for absences of three days or more.

Unused leave may carry over a maximum of **5 days** into the next calendar year.
Carry-over days must be used before the end of March or they expire.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unwell should notify their line manager before 9:30am on the first
day of absence. A self-certification form covers absences of up to 7 calendar days.
For longer absences, a fit note from a GP is required.

Sick leave is recorded separately from annual leave and does not reduce the annual
leave entitlement.

## Expenses

Reasonable business expenses must be submitted through the expenses portal within
30 days of the spend. Receipts are required for any single claim over £25.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to £35 per day without prior approval.

## Remote Working

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week by agreement with their line
manager. Remote working days must be recorded in the shared team calendar.
